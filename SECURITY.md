# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities in mdedit, or in a component it contains,
privately. Please do not open a public issue.

- **E-mail:** Obtain from GitHub
- **GitHub:** [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/rdoetjes/mdedit/security/advisories/new)
- **Post:** Phonax-Soft, Lijsterbesstraat 232, 3434AK, Nieuwegein, NL

You choose the channel. A person reads every report. Please include the
version (Help → About), your platform, the steps to reproduce and what an
attacker could achieve. Encrypted mail: ask for a key.

## What happens next

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Confirmation of receipt | 5 working days |
| First assessment and severity | 10 working days |
| Fix released, depending on severity and risk | without delay; critical issues first |
| Public advisory | when the fix is available |

We ask you to keep the issue confidential until a fix is out, or 90 days after
your report, whichever comes first; we will agree on a later date with you if a
fix needs longer. We credit reporters who want that. We won't take legal action
against research done in good faith that avoids harm to users and their data.

## Supported versions

Security updates are free. The current support period and its end date are in
[docs/cra/support-period.md](docs/cra/support-period.md). Fixes go into the latest
version.

## Advisories

Fixed vulnerabilities are published as
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/rdoetjes/mdedit/security/advisories).
